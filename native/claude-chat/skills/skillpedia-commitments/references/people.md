# People without profiling

People are explicit work relationships. Adding a person does not contact,
invite, subscribe, monitor or create an agent impersonating them. A person
record uses a stable local id, display_name, role (when supplied), optional
native account/user ID pair, timezone (when supplied), explicit work
preferences, source IDs and review date. Two people can have the same display
name. Do not merge them without a verified account identifier or user
confirmation. An alias is a proposal until confirmed.

Keep purpose and retention narrow. Do not store health, religion, political
views, family details, personality guesses, inferred emotions or employee
performance scores. A person's silence, writing style or organizational rank
is not evidence of consent, intent, reliability or acceptance of a task.
Preferences must be explicitly supplied or directly stated, with provenance;
an observed one-off reply time is not a communication preference.

Link the roster to agreed commitments and decision ownership. Separate
requester, proposed owner, confirmed owner and accountable decision maker.
Ask only about ambiguities that change the next action. On each roster change,
show add/update/remove and the source, preserve stable IDs, and write only to
an approved private store. Sharing a skill never shares the people file.

A one-to-one prep output is: shared objective; last agreed commitments; open
questions; decisions to seek; one respectful draft. Do not turn the roster
into workplace surveillance or automatic personalized persuasion.
