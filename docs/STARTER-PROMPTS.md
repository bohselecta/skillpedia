# First useful prompts

Use personal prompts only with personal/public/released inputs. In approved
corporate Claude Chat replace `hoyt-` with `skillpedia-`. In Claude Code use
`/skillpedia:<suffix>` followed by the task. Choose one skill, not all of them.

## Reset an overloaded session
Use hoyt-start. I have 30 minutes. Here is my unstructured list. Make a small
next-action window, preserve everything else, separate unknowns, and do not
send, save, schedule or assign anything.

## Triage an approved source
Use skillpedia-triage. In the approved work environment, read only the selected
channel/thread or inbox scope for the interval I provide. State which account,
what was actually read, and any missing pages or attachments. Give at most
three ordinary next actions plus every critical exception, Waiting and Needs
decision. Prepare replies in this conversation only; no external changes.

## Add a person without creating a dossier
Use hoyt-people. Add this person's explicitly supplied work role and communication
preference to the private roster I provide. Resolve native identity or leave it
unknown. Show the change first; do not contact them or infer personality traits.
Use a new approved snapshot file and preserve the prior revision.

## Track a promise
Use skillpedia-commitments on these approved notes. Separate requests, proposed
owners and agreed commitments. Capture the evidence for owner and due date;
unknown dates stay unknown. Show overdue and waiting items without inventing
completion or sending reminders.

## Research a work question
Use hoyt-research on this public technical question. Check changing claims
against current primary sources, show evidence and uncertainty, compare a
simpler alternative, and finish with the decision the research supports.

## Finish rather than just review
Use skillpedia-masterwork on the supplied status draft. Preserve its meaning,
limitations and decisions; remove repetition, finish missing in-scope wording,
and return the revised actual draft. Do not publish it or make unsupported
progress claims.

## Deliberate bridge
Use skillpedia-bridge in the approved corporate environment. Do not export
source material. Help produce a generic reusable method only if our policy and
an authorized human permit it. Show every proposed field, require exact-content
review, and otherwise provide the blank template. Do not upload anything to a
personal account. A validator PASS is not approval to disclose.
